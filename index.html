Our story

The best biryani in Avadi, cooked with patience

At Chef Bhai Biriyani in Kamaraj Nagar, Avadi, great food tells a story — one filled with heritage, love and bold flavour. Biryani is not just a meal. It is a journey through rich spices, aromatic rice and tender meat, made to bring people together.

Our recipes are inspired by tradition and perfected by cooks who care. From the vibrant Hyderabadi style to the delicate Lucknowi approach, every biryani is prepared with handpicked spices and slow-cooked in small batches.
